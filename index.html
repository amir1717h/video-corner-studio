Important notes


    

  • FFmpeg is served from this same site (no third-party CDN). The first load downloads about 30 MB, then it is cached by your browser.
    • 

  • WebM transparency depends on VP9 alpha support in the app where you use the file. MOV ProRes 4444 is widely used for alpha workflows but can be much larger.
    • 

  • For large or long videos, your browser may run out of memory. Close other tabs and try a shorter or lower-resolution copy if processing fails.
    • 

  • This page must be served over http(s) (for example on Netlify or a local web server); opening the file directly with file:// will not work.
    • 







Privacy: the selected video is processed in this browser and is never uploaded.
